A balcony overlooking a golf course is a rare asset in Cairo's short-stay market. It is not an amenity — it is the primary selling point. A room without a roof. Photography must anchor here, and it must be shot at the right moment.

Shoot at golden hour. Warm light on teak furniture against the green fairway creates a single image that stands alone among Cairo listings. Guests will book for this view before they read a word of the listing.
